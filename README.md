## 📖 About

🔹 **RimWorld — Dual Wield** is a powerful tool built to help you equip your RimWorld colonists with two weapons simultaneously for versatile dual-wield combat. Carry a pistol and machete together, or dual wield melee weapons for maximum close-quarters effectiveness.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **RimWorld — Dual Wield** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their RimWorld modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Dual Melee | Equip two melee weapons for alternating attack combos |
| Pistol Offhand | Carry a sidearm alongside a primary ranged weapon |
| Stat Penalties | Realistic accuracy and speed penalties balance dual-wield power |
| Equipment UI | Clear dual-wield equipment slots in the colonist equipment panel |
